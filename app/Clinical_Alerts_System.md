# Clinical Alerts System

## Overview

Clinical alert framework for medication safety and decision support in ICU/CCU.

## Alert Categories

### Drug Interaction Alerts

- Major interactions
- Contraindications
- Monitoring recommendations

### Dose Alerts

- Renal dose adjustment
- Maximum dose limits
- Weight-based dosing errors

### Laboratory Alerts

Examples:

- High potassium
- Low magnesium
- Elevated creatinine
- Abnormal coagulation values

### Vital Sign Alerts

Examples:

- Low MAP
- Hypotension
- Bradycardia
- Oxygen desaturation

## Alert Priority Levels

### Critical

Requires immediate clinical action.

### Warning

Requires pharmacist review.

### Information

Provides clinical guidance.

## Future Development

- Automated notifications
- Patient-specific recommendations
- AI-assisted risk prediction
