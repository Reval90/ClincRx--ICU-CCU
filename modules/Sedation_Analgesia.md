# Sedation & Analgesia Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for sedation and pain management in ICU patients.

## Sedation Agents

### Propofol

- Class: Sedative-hypnotic
- Uses:
  - Mechanical ventilation sedation
  - Procedural sedation

- Monitoring:
  - Blood Pressure
  - Heart Rate
  - Triglycerides
  - Propofol Infusion Syndrome risk

### Midazolam

- Class: Benzodiazepine
- Uses:
  - ICU sedation

- Monitoring:
  - Sedation level
  - Respiratory status
  - Delirium risk

## Analgesic Agents

### Fentanyl

- Class: Opioid analgesic
- Uses:
  - Severe pain management
  - Ventilated patients

- Monitoring:
  - Respiratory rate
  - Blood pressure
  - Pain score

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Sedation protocol review
- Dose optimization
- Pain assessment
- Adverse effect monitoring
- Drug interaction screening
