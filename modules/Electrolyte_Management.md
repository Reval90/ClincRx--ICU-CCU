# Electrolyte Management Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for electrolyte monitoring and correction in ICU/CCU patients.

## Common Electrolyte Disorders

## Potassium (K+)

Monitoring:
- Serum potassium level
- ECG changes
- Renal function

Clinical Concerns:
- Arrhythmia risk
- Digoxin toxicity risk

## Magnesium (Mg2+)

Monitoring:
- Serum magnesium
- Cardiac rhythm

Clinical Concerns:
- QT prolongation
- Arrhythmias

## Sodium (Na+)

Monitoring:
- Serum sodium
- Fluid balance

Clinical Concerns:
- Neurological complications

## Calcium (Ca2+)

Monitoring:
- Serum calcium
- Albumin level

Clinical Concerns:
- Cardiac function
- Muscle function

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Review electrolyte abnormalities
- Recommend replacement therapy
- Monitor safety parameters
- Prevent medication-related electrolyte disturbances
