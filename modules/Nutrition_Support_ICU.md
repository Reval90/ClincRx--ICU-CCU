# Nutrition Support in ICU Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for nutritional assessment and support in critically ill patients.

## Nutritional Assessment

Parameters:

- Body weight
- BMI
- Nutrition history
- Disease severity
- Metabolic status

## Nutrition Support Types

### Enteral Nutrition (EN)

Considerations:

- Early initiation when appropriate
- Feeding tolerance
- Tube medication compatibility

Monitoring:

- Gastric residuals
- Electrolytes
- Glucose control

### Parenteral Nutrition (PN)

Considerations:

- Indications
- Macronutrient requirements
- Central/peripheral access

Monitoring:

- Triglycerides
- Liver function
- Electrolytes
- Blood glucose

## Drug-Nutrition Interactions

Examples:

- Warfarin and vitamin K intake
- Phenytoin and enteral feeding
- Propofol lipid contribution

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Review nutrition prescriptions
- Monitor drug compatibility
- Optimize medication administration
- Prevent nutrition-related medication problems
