# Sepsis Management Module

## Overview

Clinical decision support module for sepsis and septic shock management in ICU.

## Initial Assessment

- Suspected infection source
- Hemodynamic status
- Organ dysfunction assessment
- Lactate measurement

## Antibiotic Management

- Early appropriate antimicrobial therapy
- Culture collection before antibiotics when possible
- Empiric therapy selection
- De-escalation based on cultures

## Hemodynamic Support

### Fluid Therapy

Monitoring:
- Blood pressure
- MAP
- Urine output
- Lactate

### Vasopressors

First-line:
- Norepinephrine

Target:
- MAP ≥ 65 mmHg

## Monitoring Parameters

- Vital signs
- Renal function
- Lactate
- Microbiology results
- Clinical response

## Clinical Pharmacist Role

- Antibiotic optimization
- Dose adjustment
- Drug compatibility review
- Monitoring and documentation
