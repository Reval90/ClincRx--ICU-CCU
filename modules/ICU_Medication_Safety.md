# ICU Medication Safety Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module focused on preventing medication errors and improving patient safety in ICU/CCU.

## High-Risk Medications

- Vasopressors
- Insulin
- Anticoagulants
- Sedatives
- Opioids
- Electrolyte replacement medications

## Safety Assessment

- Correct patient
- Correct medication
- Correct dose
- Correct route
- Correct timing

## Medication Review

Assessment:

- Indication verification
- Dose appropriateness
- Drug interactions
- Contraindications
- Monitoring requirements

## Common ICU Medication Risks

### Infusion Errors

Monitoring:
- Concentration
- Infusion rate
- Compatibility

### Duplicate Therapy

Monitoring:
- Medication reconciliation

### Renal/Hepatic Impairment

Monitoring:
- Dose adjustment requirements

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Medication reconciliation
- High-alert medication review
- Error prevention
- Documentation of interventions
