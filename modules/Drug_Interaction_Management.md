# Drug Interaction Management Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for identification and management of drug-drug interactions in ICU/CCU.

## Interaction Categories

### Pharmacokinetic Interactions

- Absorption
- Distribution
- Metabolism
- Elimination

### Pharmacodynamic Interactions

- Additive toxicity
- Increased bleeding risk
- QT prolongation
- CNS depression

## High-Risk ICU Interactions

### Anticoagulants + Antiplatelets

Risk:
- Increased bleeding

Monitoring:
- Bleeding signs
- CBC
- Clinical assessment

### Clarithromycin + DOACs

Risk:
- Increased anticoagulant effect

Monitoring:
- Bleeding risk
- Dose evaluation

### PPI + Clopidogrel

Risk:
- Reduced antiplatelet activity

Monitoring:
- Cardiovascular risk assessment

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Interaction screening
- Severity assessment
- Alternative therapy recommendation
- Monitoring plan documentation
