# Antibiotic Stewardship Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for optimization of antimicrobial therapy in ICU/CCU.

## Assessment

- Infection source
- Clinical indication
- Severity of infection
- Culture results
- Antibiotic appropriateness

## Antibiotic Review

### Selection

- Spectrum of activity
- Local resistance patterns
- Patient factors

### Dosing Optimization

- Renal dose adjustment
- Hepatic considerations
- Weight-based dosing

### Monitoring

- Clinical response
- Microbiology results
- Renal function
- Adverse effects

## Therapeutic Drug Monitoring

- Vancomycin AUC/MIC
- Aminoglycoside levels

## Clinical Pharmacist Interventions

- De-escalation recommendations
- Dose optimization
- Duration assessment
- IV to oral conversion
