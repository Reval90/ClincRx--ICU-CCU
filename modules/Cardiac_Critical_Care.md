# Cardiac Critical Care Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for management of cardiovascular conditions in ICU/CCU.

## Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)

Assessment:

- Diagnosis confirmation
- ECG findings
- Cardiac biomarkers

Medication Review:

- Antiplatelets
- Anticoagulants
- Beta blockers
- Statins

Monitoring:

- Bleeding risk
- Cardiac rhythm
- Hemodynamic status

## Heart Failure Management

Assessment:

- Ejection fraction
- Fluid status
- Renal function

Medication Classes:

- Diuretics
- ACE inhibitors/ARBs
- Beta blockers
- Inotropes

Monitoring:

- Blood pressure
- Electrolytes
- Renal function

## Arrhythmia Management

Monitoring:

- ECG
- Heart rate
- Electrolytes
- QT interval

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Medication optimization
- Interaction screening
- Dose adjustment
- Monitoring cardiovascular therapy
