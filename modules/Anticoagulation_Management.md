# Anticoagulation Management Module

## Overview

Clinical pharmacy module for anticoagulant therapy optimization in ICU/CCU patients.

## Anticoagulant Classes

### Unfractionated Heparin (UFH)

- Indications:
  - VTE prevention
  - Acute coronary syndrome
  - Thrombosis management

- Monitoring:
  - aPTT
  - Platelet count
  - Bleeding signs

### Low Molecular Weight Heparin (LMWH)

- Indications:
  - VTE prophylaxis
  - Treatment of thrombosis

- Monitoring:
  - Renal function
  - Anti-Xa when indicated

### Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs)

Examples:
- Apixaban
- Rivaroxaban

Monitoring:
- Renal function
- Drug interactions
- Bleeding risk

## Clinical Pharmacist Responsibilities

- Dose verification
- Renal dose adjustment
- Interaction screening
- Bleeding risk assessment
- Patient monitoring
