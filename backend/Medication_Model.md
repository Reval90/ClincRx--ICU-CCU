# Medication Data Model

## Medication Entity

## Basic Information

Fields:

- Drug_ID
- Drug_Name
- Generic_Name
- Drug_Class

## Clinical Information

Fields:

- Indication
- Dose
- Route
- Frequency
- Administration Instructions

## Safety Information

Fields:

- Contraindications
- Drug Interactions
- Adverse Effects
- Monitoring Parameters

## ICU Specific Data

Fields:

- High Alert Medication
- Renal Dose Adjustment
- Therapeutic Drug Monitoring Requirement
- Compatibility Information
