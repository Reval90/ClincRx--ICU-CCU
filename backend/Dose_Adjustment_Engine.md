# Dose Adjustment Engine

## Overview

Component for medication dose optimization based on patient-specific factors.

## Adjustment Factors

### Renal Function

Inputs:

- Serum Creatinine
- eGFR
- Creatinine Clearance

Applications:

- Renally cleared medications
- Toxicity prevention

## Weight-Based Dosing

Inputs:

- Body weight
- Ideal body weight
- Adjusted body weight

Applications:

- Antibiotics
- Anticoagulants
- ICU medications

## Hepatic Function

Considerations:

- Liver impairment
- Drug metabolism
- Toxicity risk

## Output

The system provides:

- Dose recommendation
- Adjustment reason
- Monitoring requirements
- Clinical alert

## Pharmacist Review

All recommendations require clinical pharmacist validation.
